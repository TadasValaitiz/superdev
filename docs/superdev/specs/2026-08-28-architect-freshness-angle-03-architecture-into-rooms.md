# Angle 3 — Architecture into rooms, and back

**Purpose:** understand how the corpus reaches an item room and how the room's discoveries return — without reading the brief template or SDD.
**Formal anchors:** D53, D57, D61; spec §5.4 (R6, R8, R11).
**Series:** 3 of 4.

> **Status guide:** LOCKED · FLEXIBLE · DEFERRED · MISMATCH.

## The central question
How does architecture travel into a fresh session without paraphrase drift — and survive contact with code that contradicts it?

## Boundaries
Starts at brief composition; ends at the checkpoint net. What the corpus contains is angles 1–2; how items get cut is angle 4.

## Concrete consequences
**Inbound — the five-part brief block (D57):** (1) as-of line naming the last reconcile SHA; (2) MAJOR reads at file:line, with the vision-not-legacy redirect wherever the map says RESHAPE/REPLACE; (3) NARROW reads on need; (4) **3–5 load-bearing rulings quoted VERBATIM** — copied from the anchors' `**LOCKED:**` claim-marker lines, each citing its D#; the ONE sanctioned duplication in the system (evidence: ten-worker O-log — verbatim quotes prevented drift where extraction did not); (5) post-SHA rulings as D# pointers only. **LOCKED.**
**The marker grammar carrying it (D53):** line-initial `**LOCKED:**` claim markers + `**Status:**` section lines, plain vocabulary, flexible payload (text, D#, link); distinguishability lives in the census script's regex, not author ceremony; MIG-MARK brackets stay code-only. **LOCKED.**
**Outbound — show must go on (D61):** a room finding the corpus contradicted collects discrepancies in its OWN item files during grounding→planning; after planning, before execution, it messages the orchestrator a summary + pointer; the orchestrator RELAYS the pointer to the architect immediately — who may act at once, in parallel with the room's execution. The room implements against reality with `MIG-MARK[MISMATCH][D#]` planted; the checkpoint is the net. **LOCKED.**

## Visible collisions
- **Verbatim quotes vs "messages are pointers":** duplication lost everywhere except here — where measured practice showed the pure-pointer alternative failing (workers drifted extracting rulings themselves).
- **LOCKED's authority vs a room that knows better:** A1 (LOCKED inviolable, stall the gutted item) lost to A2 ("show must go on") — softened by the plan-time notification seam, so the architect learns in hours, not at merge.
- **Bracket grammar vs author habit:** DOC-MARK lost 22:112 to its own author's plain-word markers; the grammar was ratified to match the hand.

## Reconciled outcome
LOCKED: brief anatomy, marker grammar, deviation flow with plan-time pointer relay. FLEXIBLE: quote-count (3–5 is guidance), notification message wording. DEFERRED (named BLIND): mid-item CORRECTION push when a quoted D# is superseded while the room runs — D57/D61 revisit hooks own it.
