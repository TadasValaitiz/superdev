# Brainstorming Angle 1 — The operator's ruling bandwidth

**Purpose:** understand why brainstorming quality is bounded by what reaches the operator's eyes — without reading the skill.
**Formal anchors:** D56, D57's evidence base (the O2 brief-anatomy lesson); the Standard amendment (re-ask in full).
**Series:** 1 of 2.

> **Status guide:** LOCKED operator-ruled · FLEXIBLE boundary agreed · MISMATCH skill text behaves differently today.

## The central question
What must a fork carry for the operator's ruling to be a real decision rather than a guess over labels?

## Boundaries
Starts where a fork is presented; ends when the ruling lands in the decision log. Angle authoring and lifecycle are angle 2.

## Concrete consequences
The failure was observed live, twice in one session: forks presented as bare A/B labels ("no idea what I am picking on"), then a re-ask that leaned on scrollback ("as presented earlier"). The ruled shape (D56 + amendment): every presented fork carries **situation (failure modes) · mechanism with a concrete example · consequences for THIS project · recommendation with reasoning**; a re-ask is a full re-presentation; labels refer, never present. The complementary compression rule: a fork that honestly fits three lines is decided by the author and logged, not presented — bandwidth is spent on few forks in full, never many forks thinly. **LOCKED.**

## Visible collisions
- **Depth vs pace:** full presentation is slow; the session's own history shows the alternative is slower — thin forks get re-asked, mis-ruled, or bounced ("something is very wrong here").
- **"One question at a time" + "multiple choice preferred" vs substance:** the old principles were the license for label-only forks; the skill's Key Principles had to be rewritten, its digraph re-drawn (the reviewer caught the digraph still encoding the old flow — a compressed control-graph outlives prose edits).
- **Self-decided trivial forks vs operator sovereignty:** resolved by the log — every self-decided fork still lands as a D# the operator can overturn.

## Reconciled outcome
LOCKED: the four-part Standard, re-ask-in-full, trivial-fork self-decision with logging. MISMATCH → fixed in d0a0e36+: digraph, Key Principles, Exploring-approaches. FLEXIBLE: the three-line triviality threshold (revisit if sessions drag).
